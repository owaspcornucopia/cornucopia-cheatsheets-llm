# AAK — Alterable authentication controls

## Non-Applicable

This threat is not selected for the current Android or iOS scenarios.

## Reasoning

AAK requires authentication controls that an attacker can influence or alter.
The clients do not have a login, biometric, or device-credential authentication control to manipulate. Client-controlled authorization and approval inputs are covered by the selected authorization cards instead.
