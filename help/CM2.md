# CM2 — Undisclosed privacy collection and sharing

## Non-Applicable

This threat is not selected for the current Android or iOS scenarios.

## Reasoning

CM2 requires a user-facing privacy disclosure and consent flow for sensitive data collection, tracking, or sharing. The scenarios use sensitive transactions in a local training application, and even though the names of the fraudsters are reported, those can not be considered to be users of the system. The app does not implement a privacy notice, tracking-domain workflow, or consent-controlled data collection flow.
